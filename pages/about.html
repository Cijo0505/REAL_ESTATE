About RealEstateAI

Who We Are

RealEstateAI is a machine learning-based house price prediction and property recommendation system.

Our platform helps users explore properties, estimate house prices and find homes based on their budget, location and personal preferences.

By combining machine learning with modern web technologies, we aim to simplify the property search process.

Our Mission

To make real estate decisions more accessible through data-driven house price estimates and personalized property recommendations.
